# NotebookLM instructions — L03: Musculoskeletal Pharmacology — Session 1

## How to use
1. In NotebookLM, create a new notebook named "L03 Musculoskeletal Pharmacology — Session 1".
2. Add sources: upload every `L03_source*.md` file in this folder (nothing else).

## 3. Quiz and flashcards
```
Use ONLY the uploaded source.
Quiz: PANCE-style. Mix about half clinical vignettes and half mechanism questions. Five answer choices (A–E), one best answer, all choices similar in length and detail. Explain why the right answer is right and why each wrong choice is wrong. Cover every topic and every learning objective; weight lecture-emphasized (bold) facts more heavily.
Flashcards: one fact per card. Front = a question or cue (drug, class, effect, or "how do you tell X from Y"). Back = the answer with its citation (slide or page). Include mechanism-to-side-effect "why" cards and discriminator cards.
```

## 4. Questions to ask the notebook
1. Walk me through the inflammatory cascade and where corticosteroids, NSAIDs, and biologics each act.
2. Why is acetaminophen not an anti-inflammatory, and how does NAPQI toxicity happen?
3. Compare COX-1 and COX-2 and explain celecoxib’s trade-off.
4. List the NSAID adverse effects by system and the four high-yield interactions, including the triple whammy.
5. Why is an NSAID a poor first choice for the 72-year-old with CKD, hypertension, and an anticoagulant?
6. Compare osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis and their treatment goals.
7. What are the three methotrexate safety rules, and what are its key toxicities?
8. Match each biologic DMARD to its target and name the shared safety risk and screening steps.
